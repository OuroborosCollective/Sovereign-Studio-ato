## 2025-08-03 - [Context-Aware Form Tooltips and Input Accessibility in Interactive Chat Workspaces]
**Learning:** For terminal-like interactive chat widgets within launcher panels (such as VPS Connector logs), providing standard ARIA roles must be paired with informative, state-dependent native tooltips. Sighted and non-sighted users alike require explicit context on why certain elements (e.g. submit button) are blocked or ready to accept text commands, and why commands require confirmation.
**Action:** Pair action inputs with unique `aria-label` attributes. Maintain dynamic, descriptive `title` attributes on conversational execute/cancel buttons to clarify the exact impact of their execution.
